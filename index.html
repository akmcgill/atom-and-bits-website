How We Work

Sounds simple, yet it's rarely easy

1

Start with the people

We've mapped thousands of workflows by looking over the shoulders of the people who know the work, not by reading process docs or SOPs. The real system lives in how people do the work, make decisions, create workarounds, and hand things off to others. Skipping this step makes all the other steps harder and more expensive.

2

Make the mess visible

People aren't great at storing and retrieving complex information without something to look at for reference. So why make things harder than they need to be? Service blueprints, process flows, decision-trees, roadmaps, mindmaps. When trade-offs are laid out where everyone can see them, the arguing stops and the deciding starts.

3

Build for the day after launch

No initiative worth its investment should be designed to "launch and leave." Build in change champions, governance and support models, and continuous improvement processes that outlast the project team. Go-live is the start of adoption, not the end of the project.
