How I work

Three habits, twenty years.

1

Start with the people

I've mapped 100+ workflows by standing on plant floors and store floors, not by reading process docs. The real system lives in how people actually work, and that's where good product decisions come from.

2

Make the mess visible

Service blueprints, RAID logs, roadmaps. When trade-offs are laid out where everyone can see them, the arguing stops and the deciding starts.

3

Build for the day after launch

Champions on the floor, training in the flow of work, support models that outlast the project team. Go-live is the start of adoption, not the end of the project.
