About

Atoms are the operational reality. Bits are the digital transformation. We work in the middle.

The name is the thesis: most modernization efforts don't fail because the technology is bad — they fail because nobody fully understood how work actually happened before trying to automate it.

Karina McGill

Founder, Product Manager & UX Architect

Karina leads discovery, requirements, and prioritization for every Atom + Bits engagement — the same work she does for clients, she does on her own projects first. Rather than treat operational discovery as a purely theoretical exercise, she tests the methodology on real, working systems before bringing it into a client engagement (see the Kitchen ERP pilot).

The approach underneath Atom + Bits: sit with the people actually doing the work, look for where the official process and the lived process quietly diverge, and write down what's true — not what's comfortable.

How we approach the work

Frontline interviews alongside leadership interviews — never one without the other. Workflow observation, not just documentation review. Active listening for the phrases that mark a real problem: "only one person knows how to do that," "the SOP says X but everyone actually does Y," "an AI tool exists but nobody trusts it."

Why Atom + Bits

We help organizations achieve operational clarity before investing heavily in modernization, AI, or automation.
