Services

We help you understand how work actually happens — before you automate it.

Most modernization efforts don't fail because the technology is bad. They fail because nobody fully understood the operational reality underneath it. Every service below exists to close that gap.

Flagship offering

Operational Readiness & Workflow Intelligence Assessment

We spend time with the people actually doing the work — frontline staff, supervisors, leadership — and watch how it really happens, not how the SOP says it does. You get a clear picture of where work breaks down, where handoffs fail, and where hidden dependencies are quietly putting your next transformation project at risk.

The rest of the toolkit

Every service solves a specific version of the same problem: nobody has the full picture.

AI Readiness Analysis

Most AI readiness assessments score your data and your tools. We score whether your organization can actually absorb the change — workflow maturity, leadership alignment, legacy knowledge risk, and trust — alongside the technical side. That's the part most frameworks skip, and it's usually the part that sinks the project.

Workflow & Dependency Mapping

A real map of how work moves through your org, including the shadow spreadsheets and workarounds nobody put in the requirements doc.

Transformation Risk Analysis

Where a rollout is likely to stall, who's likely to route around it, and what it'll cost you if those risks go unaddressed.

Operational Bottleneck & Coordination Reviews

A focused look at where approvals stall, ownership is unclear, or handoffs quietly fail — and what that's costing you.

Executive Workshops & Strategic Facilitation

Sessions built to get your leadership team looking at the same operational reality, instead of five different assumptions about it.

AI Adoption & Operational Enablement Strategy

A practical plan for rolling out AI and automation in a way people will actually use — sequenced around your real operational constraints.

How we work

Four phases. No guessing at the middle.

Every engagement follows the same backbone, scoped up or down depending on what you need.

1. Align & Prepare

Kickoff, stakeholder mapping, and a discovery plan built around your organization — not a generic template.

2. Discover

Interviews across frontline and leadership, workflow observation, and a look at where the official process and the lived process quietly diverge.

3. Analyze

Findings organized into an operational intelligence picture — friction points, transformation risks, and where AI readiness actually stands.

4. Executive Readout

A working session with leadership, recommendations sorted into quick wins, medium-term fixes, and strategic priorities.
